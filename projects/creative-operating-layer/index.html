03
Insight → positioning

From six AI tensions to one idea

AI drafted six tensions from the brief and the intelligence map. One was chosen, because it is the only one the product itself resolves.

  1. Creative lives change every few years. Their furniture is built to stay the same.
  2. They collect objects to express themselves, then store them in furniture designed to disappear.
  3. Their decks, cameras and records are their most personal things, yet they live in boxes and flight cases.
  4. They move between cities, but good furniture is too heavy and too final to move.
  5. They want pieces that feel one of a kind, but modular systems read as office furniture.
  6. They care about materials and craft, but buy online from renders they can't touch.
